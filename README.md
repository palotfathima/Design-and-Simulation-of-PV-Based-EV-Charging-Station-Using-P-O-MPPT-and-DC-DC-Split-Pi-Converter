The paper titled "Design and simulation of a solar PV-based EV Charging System using P\&O MPPT and DC-DC Split-$\pi$ Converter for Improved Battery Health" evaluated the proposed system under multiple operating conditions of varying irradiance and temperature profiles.

The results demonstrate that the MPPT algorithm effectively tracks the maximum power point under all three test scenarios, with the battery charging process follows the general CC-CV profile.

A significant part of the proposed system is the quantification of battery health using CRF and ECL. The Split-$\pi$ converter reduces current ripple to below 2\%, resulting in a considerable improvement in estimated cycle life beyond 1700 cycles and overall boosted SoH of 90\%. 

Although system efficiency appears limited due to power mismatch between the PV array and battery charging capacity, the overall proposed system demonstrates robust performance, improved battery health, and adaptability under varying environmental conditions.
